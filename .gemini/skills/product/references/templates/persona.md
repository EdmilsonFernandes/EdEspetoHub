## Guidelines for Creating Personas

### Make Them Real

- Use a specific name, not "User Type A"
- Include realistic details
- Base on actual research, not assumptions
- Make them memorable

### Focus on Relevance

Include details that inform product decisions:
- Goals that your product addresses
- Pain points your product solves
- Behaviors that affect product design
- Context that influences usage

### Avoid Stereotypes

- Don't rely on demographics alone
- Focus on behaviors and motivations
- Base on research, not assumptions
- Challenge your biases

### Keep Them Actionable

Ask: "Does this detail help us make decisions?"

**Useful**: "Prefers mobile over desktop for quick tasks"
**Not useful**: "Likes hiking on weekends"

### Primary vs Secondary

- **Primary persona**: Main user you're designing for
- **Secondary personas**: Important but not the focus
- **Negative personas**: Who you're NOT designing for

### Update Regularly

Personas should evolve as you learn more:
- After user research
- After usability testing
- When market changes
- When product pivots

### Team Alignment

Use personas in:
- Sprint planning (whose problem are we solving?)
- Design reviews (would [persona] understand this?)
- Priority discussions (which persona benefits most?)
- QA testing (test as [persona] would use it)
